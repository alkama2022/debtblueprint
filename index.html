Executive Summary

What is CollectNaija?

CollectNaija is a Nigeria-first, globally scalable SaaS platform that helps businesses track customers, invoices, debts and payments — while deploying an intelligent AI Collections Agent that communicates professionally with customers via WhatsApp, SMS, email and AI voice calls. The platform helps businesses increase successful collections, reduce manual follow-up work and maintain strong customer relationships.

₦23T+
Annual SME credit market in Nigeria (HYPOTHESIS)
40M+
Nigerian SMEs tracking debts informally
6 Phases
Structured development roadmap from MVP to global

The Core Problem

Nigerian businesses — schools, hospitals, retailers, landlords, cooperatives, lenders — are owed significant amounts of money they struggle to collect. They manage debt in notebooks, WhatsApp messages and Excel sheets. They forget to follow up. They send awkward, unprofessional reminders. Customers avoid calls. Cash flow suffers. Staff waste hours manually chasing payments.

The Solution

CollectNaija replaces informal systems with a professional, centralized platform. It automates the entire collection workflow from invoice creation through reminder scheduling to AI-powered customer communication — and provides business owners with real-time visibility into their financial position.

Key Differentiators

DifferentiatorDescription
AI-First CollectionsAn intelligent AI agent that holds real conversations, understands context, records promises and escalates when needed — not just a bulk SMS tool.
Nigeria-First UXDesigned for Android, low-bandwidth networks and WhatsApp-first communication patterns common in Nigeria.
Respectful by DesignThe AI is built to maintain customer relationships, not destroy them through harassment or threats.
Business ControlledEvery automation rule, collection policy and communication template is fully customisable by the business owner.
Financially SafeThe backend is the source of truth. The AI cannot modify financial records, confirm payments or change balances independently.
Fact
WhatsApp has over 90 million active users in Nigeria, making it the dominant business communication channel and the natural primary channel for AI-powered payment reminders.

AI Collections Agent — Overview

What the AI Agent Does

The AI Collections Agent is an intelligent assistant that manages customer payment communications on behalf of businesses. It is not a chatbot — it is a controlled, policy-governed communication engine with conversational capability.

AI Agent Capabilities

  • Monitor outstanding invoices and due dates
  • Send contextual, professional payment reminders
  • Engage in natural conversations via WhatsApp
  • Understand customer responses (promises, disputes, objections)
  • Record promise-to-pay with date and amount
  • Explain invoice details using verified backend data
  • Send payment links
  • Handle simple objections and FAQ
  • Detect when human escalation is needed
  • Pause communications when requested
  • Log every action for audit

AI Agent Hard Limits

  • Cannot change invoice amounts
  • Cannot confirm payment without backend verification
  • Cannot create or waive fees
  • Cannot reveal another customer's data
  • Cannot contact beyond configured limits
  • Cannot threaten or harass
  • Cannot invent payment plans
  • Cannot fabricate balances or deadlines
  • Cannot make legal claims
  • Cannot ignore opt-out requests

Inbound Webhook Processing

# Webhook handler pattern — idempotent, safe
def handle_paystack_webhook(request):
    # 1. Verify signature
    sig = request.headers.get('X-Paystack-Signature')
    if not verify_hmac_sha512(request.body, sig, PAYSTACK_SECRET):
        return HttpResponse(status=401)

    payload = json.loads(request.body)
    event_id = payload['id']   # unique per event from Paystack

    # 2. Idempotency check
    if WebhookEvent.objects.filter(provider_event_id=event_id).exists():
        return HttpResponse(status=200)  # already processed

    # 3. Record raw event
    event = WebhookEvent.objects.create(
        provider='paystack', provider_event_id=event_id,
        event_type=payload['event'], payload=payload
    )

    # 4. Dispatch to Celery task — async processing
    process_payment_webhook.delay(event.id)
    return HttpResponse(status=200)  # return 200 immediately
Requirement
Webhook handlers must return HTTP 200 within 5 seconds. All actual processing happens asynchronously in Celery tasks. Webhook events are stored before processing to support replay on failures.

# Runs every hour via Celery Beat
def schedule_reminders_for_invoice(invoice):
    policy = invoice.org.collection_policy
    today = date.today()

    for days_offset in policy.reminder_intervals_days:
        trigger_date = invoice.due_date + timedelta(days=days_offset)

        if trigger_date != today:
            continue

        # Policy checks
        if not is_within_allowed_hours(policy, invoice.org.timezone):
            queue_for_morning(invoice, days_offset); continue

        if contacts_today_for_customer(invoice.customer) >= policy.max_contacts_per_day:
            continue

        if invoice.customer.opt_out:
            log_skipped_opt_out(invoice); continue

        if reminders_sent_count(invoice) >= policy.max_reminders:
            create_escalation_ticket(invoice); continue

        # All checks passed — queue message
        channel = select_optimal_channel(invoice.customer, policy, days_offset)
        queue_reminder.delay(invoice.id, channel, days_offset)

Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA 2023)

  • Businesses using CollectNaija to process customer data are data controllers. CollectNaija is a data processor.

  • CollectNaija does not hold customer funds — payments flow through licensed payment providers (Paystack, Flutterwave).
  • CollectNaija is acting as a SaaS tool, not a payment service provider. No CBN PSP licence is required in this model. (Confirm with legal counsel.)

Debt Collection Ethics & Legal

  • AI must not make false legal claims (e.g., "we will sue you" without authorisation from the business).

  • Businesses using CollectNaija are responsible for ensuring their collection activity complies with applicable laws.
